    My Story

    Warning: If you are looking for an exciting and motivating story, you are on the right track!

    Ever since I was a child, I have been captivated by the world of technology. I remember spending hours tinkering with computers and exploring the endless possibilities they offered. This curiosity is driving me to pursue a career in technology, where I plan to combine my passion for problem-solving with my love for innovation.

    Everyone says those lines, but what makes this different? Are you guys thinking like that? Am I really right?

    Let me tell you: everything started back in the 6th grade when I fell in love—not with girls, but with a girl. I'm a loyal guy, so why would it be plural? I consider myself quite the romantic, yet I don't have a single female friend, let alone a "female best friend." And definitely no girlfriend.

    But here is how I learned programming. I might have sounded like a romantic just now, but I'm about to motivate you. Listen, the laptop I am using right now is 16 years old. It is actually 2 to 3 years older than I am! It's a 2010 model, likely purchased around that time. It only has 3 GB of RAM, no dedicated GPU, and yet here I am, running Windows 10 and VS Code on it. The laptop is probably screaming at me.

    But it’s all about the situation. I realized that if I blame my assets and complain that I don't have a good laptop or a powerful computer, I would never be able to learn programming. Even though it gets incredibly difficult—and I sometimes have to switch to using my phone—I keep going. Through this, I learned a vital lesson: never blame your assets or your circumstances. Just start with what you have, and you will achieve things far better than you ever imagined.

    Anyway, let me head out because I have a ton of work to do, and you should get back to yours, too. Bye! Bye!
